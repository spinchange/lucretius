FINDINGS
1068	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.60	"aut ubi eos lactant, pedibus morsuque potentes" (1068, with 1069) describes the dogs fondling their pups, though strong in paw and bite, while they mimic gulps with teeth held back; the English turns lactant into "toss them with their paws" and potentes into the action "snapping at them", which loses the concession that gives the line its point; fix: "or when they fondle them, strong as they are in paw and bite,"
1055	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.55	"vocis inauditos sonitus" means sounds of voice never heard before, i.e. unfamiliar and unintelligible, but the English "unheard sounds" says the sounds are not heard at all, which with the deaf (1052) misleads; fix: "unheard-of sounds of voice to batter their ears in vain."
