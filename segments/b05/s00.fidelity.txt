FINDINGS
5.53	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.75	"ipsis iam mortalibus e divis dare dicta suerit" says he was used to give sayings from the gods to mortals themselves. The English drops the recipients and the verb "dare" and substitutes a conjectural "immortalibus" for the printed "mortalibus", giving "many sayings about the immortal gods themselves"; fix: "to give to mortals themselves, from the gods, many sayings" (this also removes the {{NOTE}} emendation).
SECONDARY
5.58	REGISTER	MINOR	0.50	"aevi" (register: aevum = "life, age") is rendered "time" in "the strong laws of time", although 5.61 renders "aevum" as "ages"; fix: "the strong laws of age" or "of life's span".
5.72	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.50	"vesci per nomina rerum" means to make use of, or feed on, the names of things among themselves. "Deal with one another" loses the "use" sense; fix: "began to use among themselves the names of things".
