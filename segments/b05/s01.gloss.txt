156-165: Lat. Dicere [gods voluisse parare mundi naturam; decere laudare opus, putare aeternum; nec fas esse sollicitare (active inf) quod fundatum sit... nec vexare, evertere]; adfingere, addere: desiperest. FINDINGS
5.160	GRAMMAR	MODERATE	0.70	"nec fas esse... sollicitare... nec verbis vexare et ab imo evertere" (160-163) has active infinitives governing "quod... fundatum" (what was founded), but the English "it is sin that what... was founded... ever to be shaken... or harried... and overturned" puts them into a passive "that"-clause with no finite verb, so the sentence does not construe; recast 160 as "and that it is sin to disturb what, by the gods' ancient reasoning," with 162 "ever, by any force, from its seat," and 163 "or harry it with words and overturn it from top to bottom,".
5.193	GRAMMAR	MINOR	0.55	"deciderunt quoque et in talis venere meatus" pairs two perfects, but the English gives "fell" (past) and then "come" (present) in the same "if"-clause (192-193); "and have come, as well, into such courses"
