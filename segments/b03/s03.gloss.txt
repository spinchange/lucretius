FINDINGS
539	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.58	In "qui quoniam nusquamst ... dilaniata foras dispargitur, interit ergo" the subject changes from locus (qui, 538) to the spirit (dilaniata, feminine), but the English "and since it is nowhere ... torn to pieces it is scattered abroad, and therefore perishes" keeps the same "it", so it reads as the place that is torn and perishes; suggested fix: "the spirit, torn to pieces, is scattered abroad, and therefore perishes."
493	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.55	"spumans" is nominative and agrees with "vis morbi" (492), not with accusative "animam", but "drives and troubles the spirit, foaming," attaches the foaming to the spirit; suggested fix: "foaming, drives and troubles the spirit, as on the salt sea"
