FINDINGS
3.69	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.70	"se effugisse... longe longeque remosse" has reflexive se (wish to have escaped and removed themselves far, far away), but the English "removed them far, far away" makes the evils the object; "to have escaped from these and removed themselves far, far away,"
3.86	GRAMMAR	MODERATE	0.60	"homines patriam carosque parentis / prodiderunt" is plain active perfect, but "men have their country and dear parents / betrayed" reads as causative (had others betray them); 3.85 "for often before now men have betrayed their country and dear parents," with 3.86 "seeking to shun the precincts of Acheron."
3.31	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.60	"cunctarum exordia rerum" is rendered "the beginnings of all things", principia's term, though the register fixes exordia = origins at 3.31 (and 4.26 repeats this line as "origins"); "And since I have taught what the origins of all things"
3.90	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.60	"quae pueri in tenebris pavitant finguntque futura" departs from the fixed refrain 2.58 (the note on this line demands verbatim); "what children in the dark shudder at and imagine will come."
3.87	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.55	"omnia... metuunt" is "fear all things" where the fixed refrain 2.55 has "fear everything"; "For just as children tremble and fear everything"
3.131	OMISSION	MINOR	0.50	"animae quasi pars hominis" hedges with quasi ("as it were a part of man"), which the English drops, asserting flatly "to be a part of man"; "and of the spirit, to be as it were a part of man, give back the name"
