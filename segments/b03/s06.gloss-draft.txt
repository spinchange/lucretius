ANALYSIS

**819-823**
- Latin: si [anima] habenda est inmortalis (gerundive, pres. subj. sense) | quod munita tenetur (pres. ind. pass.) **ab vitalibus rebus**, agent or means = life-giving things | aut quia non veniunt (neg.) aliena salutis | aut quia quae veniunt recedunt pulsa, prius quam sentire queamus quid noceant.
- English: if it should be held immortal | that it is kept fortified **against things hostile to life** | because foreign things do not come | or those that come are driven back and retreat before we can feel the harm.
- DIFF: vitalibus ("life-giving, vital") becomes "hostile to life". The protectors become the threat.

**824-827**
- Latin: praeter quam quod aegret (subj.) cum morbis corporis | advenit id quod eam macerat, male habet, fatigat | peccata remordent (pres.) praeteritis male admissis.
- English: besides the fact that it sickens along with the diseases | there comes that which torments, keeps wretched, wears out | its sins gnaw for wrongs committed.
- MATCH

**828-829**

- English: the same.
- MATCH

**830-831**

- English: the same.
- MATCH

**832-842**
- Latin: ut nihil aegri sensimus (perf.), venientibus Poenis (abl. abs.), cum omnia contremuere (perf.), in dubio fuere utrorum ad regna cadendum esset omnibus humanis | sic, ubi non erimus, cum discidium fuerit (fut. perf.) corporis atque animai, quibus e sumus apti | haud quicquam nobis accidere poterit (fut.) sensumque movere | non si terra mari miscebitur.
- English: just as we felt no distress when the Carthaginians were coming, when all things shuddered, it stood in doubt under which rule all men must fall | so when we shall not be, when the parting has come | nothing will be able to happen to us or move our sense | not if earth should be mingled with sea.
- MATCH. "all men … all that is human" doubles omnibus humanis, but the sense holds.

**843-846**
- Latin: et si iam sentit (sg.) [animi natura animaeque potestas] postquam distractast | nil tamen est ad nos, qui consistimus comptu coniugioque … apti.
- English: even if they feel after they are torn away | still it is nothing to us, who exist by the joining and wedlock.
- MATCH
