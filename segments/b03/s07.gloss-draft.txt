FINDINGS
1040	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.90	"memores motus ... mentis" is rendered "the mindful motions of his mind", but the register fixes memores motus as "the remembering motions" (3.1040, explicitly "not mindful"); "warned him that the remembering motions of his mind were slackening,"
1044	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.88	"exortus ut aetherius sol" is rendered "as the sun does, risen in the upper air", but the register fixes aetherius sol as "the sun in the ether" (5.215; "never 'the sun of the upper air'"); "all the stars, as the sun in the ether does when it has risen."
