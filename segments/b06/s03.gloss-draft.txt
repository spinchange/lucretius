FINDINGS
384	LINEATION	MINOR	0.55	Latin 384-385 ("quo pacto per loca saepta / insinuarit, et hinc dominatus ut extulerit se") puts insinuarit in 385, but the English moves it into 384 and pads 385 with "there" and "again"; it also departs from the register's verbatim 6.91-93 = 6.383-385 refrain. Fix: "it has turned from here, in what way through walled-in places" / 385 "it has slipped in, and how, having held sway, it has carried itself out,"
460	GRAMMAR	MINOR	0.50	"tanto magis edita fument" has one correlative (the nearer, the more they smoke) with edita as a participle, "lofty"; the English "the more they smoke, the higher they stand" makes edita a second comparative clause. Fix: "the more, lofty as they are, they smoke"
