FINDINGS
851	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.60	"partim" (850-851) means "some of them" with homines ("and some suppose that the fierce sun beneath the earth heats it"), but the English reads it as an adverb on fervescere, "it partly grows hot", and makes all men hold the belief; fix 850-851: "At this spring men marvel too much, and some suppose that by the fierce / sun beneath the earth it grows hot"
830	MISTRANSLATION	MINOR	0.50	"interdum" qualifies "fit ut ... discutiat" (sometimes the force drives apart the air), but the English leaves the dispersal unqualified in 830-831 and attaches "at times" to the result in 832; fix: "It happens too at times that this force and exhalation of Avernus" (with 832 "so that the place there is left almost empty")
800	AMBIGUITY	MINOR	0.50	"plenior" is nominative and agrees with the bather (fuller, after a meal), but "too full" opening 800 after "hot baths" in 799 reads as describing the baths; fix: "with a full stomach, and bathe, how easily, in the tub of boiling water,"
