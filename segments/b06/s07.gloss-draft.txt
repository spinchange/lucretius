FINDINGS
908	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.65	"quem Magneta vocant ... Grai" is rendered "the magnet", against the register's fixed "the Magnes" for 6.908 (where "magnet" is allowed only in the notes), and it loses the proper-name sense that 909 explains; "the stone the Greeks call the Magnes, from its native name,"
956	GRAMMAR	MODERATE	0.55	The ablative absolute "tempestate ... coorta" (singular) is made the plural subject "storms" of "facessunt" in 957, whose real subject is the unexpressed feminine plural "remotae"; "and when a storm has arisen in earth and sky," (with 957 "they withdraw, far off, into sky and earth, as is right;")
926	AMBIGUITY	MINOR	0.50	"exesor moerorum" is nominative, in apposition to "aestus" (925), but the English places "devourer of walls" right after "the sea", where it attaches to the sea (if changed, change 4.220 as well, to keep the refrain); "of the sea, the spray that devours walls, along the shores;"
