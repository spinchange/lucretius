ANALYSIS
1002-1007: L: est (pres. ind.) natura loci spatiumque profundi; rel. quod (obj.) neque fulmina (subj.) percurrere possint (pres. subj., result-like) [labentia → fulmina; perpetuo tractu abl.] nec facere ut restet minus ire meando; usque adeo patet copia rebus, finibus exemptis (abl. abs.). E: there is a nature of place and space; such that thunderbolts could not run through it, gliding through everlasting tract; nor bring it about that less remains; so widely lies open a huge room, its boundaries taken away. MATCH
1008-1016: L: natura (subj.) tenet ne summa ipsa sibi modum parare possit; quae (natura) cogit corpus finiri inane (abl.), inane finiri corpore; ut alternis omnia infinita reddat, aut alterutrum, nisi terminet alterum, pateat inmoderatum; [sense break] nec mare… possent (impf. subj.) sistere tempus. E: nature herself prevents the sum from providing itself a limit; she compels body bounded by void, void by body; so that she renders all infinite; or one of the two, if the other did not bound it, would stretch out unmeasured; [note on break] nor sea… could hold their ground. MATCH (ipsa with natura is defensible; the conditional "would" for the result-clause pateat sits within the noted break)
1017-1020: L: copia (subj.) dispulsa de coetu ferretur soluta per inane; sive numquam concreta creasset ullam rem, quoniam cogi disiecta nequisset. E: the store, driven from its union, would be carried, set loose; or would never have come together and created anything, since it could not have been driven together. MATCH
1021-1034: L: neque primordia (subj.) consilio se locarunt (perf.) mente sagaci, nec pepigere quos motus quaeque darent; sed quia multa mutata vexantur percita plagis, experiundo omne genus motus et coetus deveniunt in disposituras qualibus summa consistit creata; et (summa) servata per annos, ut semel coniecta est in motus convenientis, efficit ut amnes integrent mare, terra fota novet fetus, gens summissa floreat, ignes vivant. E: same subjects, negations, tenses; "it" = the sum brings about rivers replenish, earth renews, race flourishes, fires live. MATCH
