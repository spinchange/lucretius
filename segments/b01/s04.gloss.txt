FINDINGS
1.627	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.55	"illa quoque esse ... solida atque aeterna" turns from the least parts (quae, 626) to the first-beginnings built from them, but the English "they too" resumes "these" and makes the partless least things the subject; suggested fix: "you must admit that those, too, are solid and eternal."
