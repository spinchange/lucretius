FINDINGS
766	MISTRANSLATION	CRITICAL	0.80	"nec dissentit eum mortis letique potitum / iam pridem, quem mens vivom se cernere credit" (766-767) says memory does not object that the man the mind sees alive is long dead; the English adds "is not" and makes the unprotested claim "this dead one is not the one seen alive", which reverses the identity. Fix: "and does not protest that this one is in the grip of death and doom / long since, the one the mind believes it sees alive."
776	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.85	"copia particularum" is rendered "particles", which the register bars (particulae 4.261, 4.776 = small part, tiny parts), and the note wrongly says the word occurs only here. Fix: "the abundance of tiny parts, that it can supply them." Also drop or correct the note.
648	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.55	"proinde et seminibus constant variantque figura" means the creatures consist of seeds that differ in shape; "vary in their shape" reads as the creatures' outward shape, already stated in 646-647. Fix: "so too they consist of seeds that vary in their shape."
636	GRAMMAR	MINOR	0.50	"tantaque in his rebus distantia differitasque est" is an indicative main assertion outside the scope of quareve (634-635); "and why in these things there is such distance" subordinates it to the promise to explain. Fix: "and in these things there is such distance and difference"
