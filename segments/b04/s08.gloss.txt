E: it is right to flee, to scare away, to turn, to cast, and not hold it back, "and so lay up for yourself care". DIFF: the negation's reach over servare is unclear; the line can be read as one more thing it is right to do.

FINDINGS
1067	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.60	The Latin puts both "nec retinere" (1066) and "servare sibi curam certumque dolorem" under one negation, but "and so lay up for yourself care and certain pain" can be read as another thing it is "right" to do, which would reverse the advice; "and so not lay up for yourself care and certain pain;"
1158	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.60	"quoniam foedo adflictentur amore" is a subjunctive giving the mockers' reason about the others they mock, but "since they are afflicted" states it as fact with an unclear "they", which blurs the irony of 1159 (they mock others' love and miss their own); "Venus, since, they say, the others are afflicted by a shameful love,"
1141	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.50	"in amore ... proprio" means love that is one's own, possessed and secure (as opposed to "adverso" in 1142), but "true" suggests sincere love instead; "And these evils are found in love that is your own and at its most"
