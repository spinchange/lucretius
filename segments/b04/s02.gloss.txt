318-323
L: credas (pres subj, potential, 2sg) simulacra indugredi pariter pedemque ponere nobiscum gestumque imitari, propterea quia, qua parte speculi recedas, continuo nequeunt simulacra illinc reverti; quandoquidem natura cogit omnia referri ac resilire ab rebus reddita ad aequos flexus.
E: images walk forward in step with us, and set their foot down with ours, you would believe, and imitate our gesture, because from whatever part you withdraw the images cannot return; since nature compels all things to be carried back and leap back at equal angles.
DIFF: the governing credas is placed after the first clause, which then reads as a flat assertion.

370-374
L: nimirum, quia terra locis ex ordine certis privatur lumine solis quacumque meantes officimus, repletur item quod eius liquimus, propterea fit uti videatur [ea] quae fuit umbra corporis, eadem (nom fem, short final a by metre: ōn(e) ĕă | dēm, agrees with quae/umbra), e regione (adverbial, "straight on") usque secuta nos.
E: because earth is deprived of light where we get in its way and refilled where we have left it, what was the shadow of the body seems to have followed us all along "in the same direction".
DIFF: eadem read as ablative with regione ("the same direction"); the Latin says the shadow seems to be the same one following us straight on, the point taken up at 381-382.

FINDINGS
374	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.75	"e regione eadem nos usque secuta": eadem is nominative (short a by metre) agreeing with quae...umbra, "the same shadow", and e regione is adverbial "straight on", but the English reads eadem with regione as "in the same direction", losing the claim that it seems to be the same shadow, which 4.381-382 (umbraque quae fuit hic eadem) then disputes; "seems to have followed us straight on all the while, the same."
318	AMBIGUITY	MINOR	0.50	"Indugredi ... credas" (with 319): credas governs all three infinitives, but the English opens 318 with the flat statement "images walk forward in step with us" and only then adds "you would believe", so the first proposition can be read as asserted fact; "Further, you would believe that images walk forward in step, and set"
