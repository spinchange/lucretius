FINDINGS
1043	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.85	"in loca… nervorum certa" is rendered "fixed regions of the nerves", against the register's binding nervi = sinews (listed for 4.1043); "gathering into fixed regions of the sinews, and stirs"
1115	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.85	"nervis coniecta cupido" is rendered "the desire gathered in the nerves", against the register's nervi = sinews (listed for 4.1115); "At last, when the desire gathered in the sinews has burst out,"
1088	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.65	"quod fieri contra totum natura repugnat" means nature resists this happening, quite the contrary; the English drops "quod fieri" and invents "the whole" as a subject that "goes the opposite way"; "But nature fights against this coming to pass, wholly the contrary;"
1157	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.55	"alios alii inrident… suadent" sets some mocking others, and the mockers are the ones in 1159 who do not see their own ills; "laugh at one another and urge each other" makes it reciprocal and loses the irony of 1158-1159; "And some men laugh at others and urge them to appease"
1067	AMBIGUITY	MODERATE	0.55	"et servare sibi curam" stands under the nec of 1066 (not to keep care and pain), but "and so lay up for yourself care and certain pain" can be read as one more thing it is "right" to do; "and so store up for yourself care and certain pain." → "and so not store up for yourself care and certain pain;"
1141	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.50	"in amore… proprio" means love that is one's own and secure (requited), not "true"; the English shifts the contrast with "adverso atque inopi" in 1142; "And these evils are found in love that is secure and at its most"
