FINDINGS
727	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.60	"concursus" in the list of 2.725-727 is rendered "meetings", which collapses the impact-word into the register's rendering of coetus/concilium and breaks the fixed "collisions" (2.1021, 1.685); fix: "collisions, motions; which not only divide living bodies"
719	MISTRANSLATION	MINOR	0.55	"quaedam ratio res terminat omnis" says "a certain principle", but the English "the same principle" follows the emendation eadem rather than the source text; fix: "by these laws, a certain principle bounds all things."
724	AMBIGUITY	MINOR	0.55	"non volgo paria omnibus omnia constant" means all things are not composed alike with all, but "they are not all made alike of all" reads as things made out of all, and it departs from the fixed refrain of 2.337 (2.723 also has "too few" for the refrain's "few"); fix: "but because, as a rule, not all are alike to all."
694	AMBIGUITY	MINOR	0.50	The same line, "non volgo paria omnibus omnia constant", is rendered "they are not all made alike of all", which can be read as composition out of all and breaks the refrain fixed as 2.337 = 2.694; fix: "but because, as a rule, not all are alike to all."
