FINDINGS
2.364	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.80	"vitulorum aliae species" is rendered "other shapes of calves", against the register's fixed "sight" for species of animals (first at 2.364, "never shapes", which is figura); "nor can the sight of other calves in the glad pastures"
2.333	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.80	"exordia rerum" is rendered "starting-points", but the register fixes exordia = "origins" with first use at this line ("starting-point" only in non-technical 1.149); "Come now, next learn of what sort the origins"
2.336	MISTRANSLATION	MAJOR	0.75	"non quo multa parum simili sint praedita forma" (register: few have a like form) becomes "not that many are furnished with forms too little alike", i.e. not that many are unlike, which reverses the clause and breaks the non quo / sed quia contrast with 2.337; it also departs from the fixed refrain (= 2.723, 2.694); "not that few are endowed with a like form,"
2.337	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.60	"non volgo paria omnibus omnia constant" is rendered "they are not all alike, each to each" instead of the fixed refrain text shared with 2.724 and 2.694; "but because, as a rule, not all are alike to all."
2.383	GRAMMAR	MODERATE	0.55	"fluat", whose subject is "fulmineus ignis" (2.382), is given to our fire ("ours, which flows from torches"), and "terrestribus" (modifying taedis) is detached as "born of the earth"; "than ours, born from torches of the earth, flows forth;" with 2.382 "why the fire of the thunderbolt flows far more piercing"
