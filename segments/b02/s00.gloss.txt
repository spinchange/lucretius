FINDINGS
2.23	LEXICAL	MODERATE	0.55	"neque natura ipsa requirit" uses requirit absolutely, meaning nature does not feel the want of (the statues, silver, gilded ceilings in 2.24-28), but the English "nor does nature herself require" leaves the verb objectless and gives the wrong sense; "more welcome now and then; nor does nature herself feel any want,"
2.94	GRAMMAR	MINOR	0.50	"quoniam spatium sine fine modoquest / inmensumque patere ... pluribus ostendi" makes the infinitive patere depend on ostendi, but the English (2.92-94) gives "since space is without end..., and spreads... / I have shown..." with nothing linking the clauses to "I have shown", so the sentence does not parse; "as I have shown in many words and proved by sure reasoning."
