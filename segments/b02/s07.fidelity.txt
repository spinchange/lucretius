FINDINGS
1104	MISTRANSLATION	MODERATE	0.76	"indignos inque merentes" means the undeserving of such a fate and those who deserve well (the innocent and the deserving), but "the unworthy and the undeserving" makes both groups morally bad and drops the contrast with "nocentes"; "and strikes dead the innocent and the deserving?"
SECONDARY
1093	LEXICAL	MINOR	0.55	"pro sancta deum tranquilla pectora pace" has "sancta" modifying "pace" and "tranquilla" modifying "pectora", but the English gives "holy hearts" and "tranquil peace"; "For by the tranquil hearts of the gods, in holy peace"
